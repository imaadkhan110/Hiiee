A letter I probably couldn't say out loud

Where do I even begin?

I don't really know how to put everything I feel into words. There are some people who enter your life quietly, without announcing how important they're going to become. And then, somehow, one day you realize they've become part of the way you think, the things you remember, and the little moments you wish you could keep forever.

That's what you became to me. Not just another person. Not just another name in my phone. You became someone whose presence could change the entire mood of a day without even trying.

Maybe that's the strange thing about caring about someone. You don't always notice it happening. It happens slowly. A conversation here. A laugh there. A random memory that suddenly makes you smile. And eventually, that person starts meaning something you can't really measure.

If you ever wonder whether you matter to me, I hope you remember this: you do. More than I probably know how to explain properly. And even if life changes, people change, and the future takes us places we can't predict, I'll always be grateful that our paths crossed.

I don't want this page to promise things that nobody can predict. I just wanted it to exist. A tiny reminder that somewhere in this enormous world, there is a person who thought you were special enough to build an entire little website for.

With all my heart.
